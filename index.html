How it works

  • Each row is one continuous copper strip; Cut breaks it between holes.
  • Click two holes for a wire; click once for components.
  • Right-click or R rotates the next component.
  • In Select mode: click a component to inspect, drag to move, hover a pin for its name.
  • Use Erase to remove components or cuts.

Shortcuts

  • V select   W wire   L LED
  • N NPN   R resistor / rotate
  • D diode   X cut   E erase
  • Ctrl+Z undo   Esc cancel
